WorldNewsHub Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

Welcome to WorldNewsHub. This Privacy Policy explains how WorldNewsHub handles information when you use our mobile application.

WorldNewsHub is a global newspaper directory and discovery application that helps users find newspaper websites from countries around the world. The application provides links to newspaper websites and may display those websites using an in-app WebView.

In short: WorldNewsHub does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

1. Information We Collect

WorldNewsHub is designed to operate without requiring users to provide personal information.

We do not intentionally collect or store:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your contacts
  • Your precise location
  • Your photographs or videos
  • Your account credentials
  • Your payment information
  • Your personal messages

2. Newspaper Websites and Third-Party Content

WorldNewsHub contains links to newspaper websites operated by independent third parties.

When you open a newspaper website through WorldNewsHub, you may interact directly with that third-party website. The newspaper website may collect information according to its own privacy policy, cookies policy, and terms of service.

WorldNewsHub does not control how third-party newspaper websites collect, use, store, or process information.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

3. WebView Usage

WorldNewsHub may use an in-app WebView to display newspaper websites. Content displayed inside the WebView is provided by the respective third-party website.

Any information collected by a newspaper website through its website, cookies, advertising technologies, analytics services, or other mechanisms is governed by that website's own privacy policy.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

WorldNewsHub itself does not intentionally use cookies to identify individual users.

However, third-party newspaper websites opened through the application may use cookies, local storage, analytics technologies, advertising technologies, or similar mechanisms.

Such technologies are controlled by the respective third-party website and are subject to its privacy practices.

5. Analytics and Advertising

WorldNewsHub does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or behavioral profiling.

WorldNewsHub does not currently include third-party advertising networks within the application.

If analytics, advertising, or other third-party services are added in a future version of the application, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

6. Data Sharing

WorldNewsHub does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Because WorldNewsHub does not require users to create an account or provide personal information, we do not have a database of user profiles or personal information to sell or share.

Information may be transmitted directly to third-party newspaper websites when you choose to open those websites. Such interactions are governed by the privacy policies of the respective third parties.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information handled by third-party websites or services.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

WorldNewsHub does not maintain user accounts or intentionally store personal information about users.

Therefore, there is generally no personal account data stored by WorldNewsHub that requires deletion.

Data that may be stored or processed by third-party newspaper websites is controlled by those websites. To request deletion of information held by a third-party newspaper website, please contact that website directly.

9. Children's Privacy

WorldNewsHub is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

10. External Links

WorldNewsHub contains links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of external websites.

Once you leave WorldNewsHub and access an external website, that website's privacy policy applies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, applicable laws, or our data practices.

Any changes will be published on this page. The Last Updated date at the top of this page will also be updated when changes are made.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of WorldNewsHub, please contact us.
